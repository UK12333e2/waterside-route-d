Six integration factories, opened as the gates allow

Every high-risk decision leaves a plain-English record that a tenant, the ombudsman or an insurer can read in one click. A software vendor can't hand this over, because it doesn't own the decision. France: the first export

The operations plan takes Route D to France from 2029: 20k homes in 2029, 60k in 2030 and 100k in 2031, seeded with £15m. France has more private rentals than the UK, but lower fees, rent control in 69 towns and a free state rent guarantee. So the product lines carry more of the value there than they do at home.

What changes the Route D case in France

Lower fees. Landlords pay 5–10% of rent for management, and estimate a fair price at 8%; we add about 1.5 points for letting fees spread over a tenancy Galian 2026, against 12% in the case's UK model. Each French home earns less from lettings.

A bigger, under-served base. About 23% of France's 31.9m main homes are let by private landlords INSEE 2026p, roughly 7.4m homes. Only about 48% of landlords use a professional manager Galian 2026.

Rent control caps lettings, not products. Rent control applies in 69 towns and was extended to 31 July 2027. That limits what a home can earn from rent, but not what Dwelly earns from protection, finance and data.

The French product wedge: renovation finance

Homes rated G on the energy certificate (DPE) can no longer be let since 1 January 2025; F follows in 2028 and E in 2034. About 1.1m private rentals are rated F or G ONRE via EDF.

That makes landlord renovation finance, with the works arranged through Dwelly's contractor network, the natural lead product in France. Rent guarantees are harder: Visale, the free state guarantee, wrote 350,000 contracts in 2025, and a paid guarantee cannot be combined with a guarantor.

Why the US is where it's at

About 46m renter households, a record 22.7m of them cost-burdened Harvard JCHS 2026. Most rentals are owned by individuals, with 17.7m individual landlords holding about 48m units RHFS via VerticalRent.

Management is even more fragmented than in the UK: about 340,000 property-management businesses in a $139.9bn industry IBISWorld 2026, with no firm above 5%. That is the roll-up thesis at about eight times the UK's scale.

Why the US punishes careless AI

RealPage settled with the DOJ in November 2025. It must stop training on real-time confidential rent data, and ten states kept their own case going. California and New York have passed laws against rent-setting software.

SafeRent paid about $2.3m and stopped scoring voucher holders after a Fair Housing Act case. Dwelly's rule that a person signs off and every decision is logged, built for the UK ombudsman, is the licence to operate here.

Method and checks

Data

Homes: Census 2021 TS054, households renting from a private landlord or letting agency, 318 council areas (April 2023 boundaries) ONS/Nomis.

Rent: Price Index of Private Rents, average monthly rent, September 2026, released 21 Oct 2026 ONS. City of London and Isles of Scilly have no figure; the regional median is used and flagged.

Agencies: UK Business Counts 2026, local units in SIC 68310 (real estate agencies) and 68320 (management of real estate), by employment size ONS/Nomis. Counts are rounded to 5 by ONS.

France: OLL network 2026 edition (2024 median rents, 3-room flats); INSEE tenure 2026p; rent control per pap.fr and moneyvox (Aug 2026); département boundaries via france-geojson. US: Harvard JCHS 2026, IBISWorld 2026, state licensing summaries, DOJ/RealPage (Nov 2025), SafeRent (Nov 2024); state boundaries US Census via us-atlas.

Dwelly: brands and homes from the case (Ex.2) and Dwelly's site; towns from trade press. Plotted at the council area containing the office.

Formulas

fee pool = homes × rent × 12 × fee%

value per home = lettings revenue × 5 + new-line revenue × 9.4, the basis of our deck. Lettings revenue = rent × 12 × fee; protection = revenue per attached home × attach, scaled by rent; financing scaled by rent; data and advisory flat per home.

year plan: the deck timeline (UK and France homes, deals, factories, take-up, financing, data, advisory, funding). Value in year t = homes × £2,800 × 5 + (protection + financing + data + advisory) × 9.4, on the deck’s 2031 multiples.

gates: miss Gate 1, pace holds at 13 deals a year and France waits; miss Gate 2, protection stays partner-only at 20% take-up, no financing, no France; miss Gate 3, pace holds at 28 deals a year and France stays a 20k pilot.

price = rent × 12 × fee × pre-margin × multiple (case Ex.6: £2.1k a home)

need = size − Dwelly homes in catchment, must fit the share cap; hubs chosen greedily by (size × value − need × price) ÷ (need × price), weighted towards hubs that hold enough homes themselves.

Checks we ran

Limits: Census homes are from 2021 and the private rented sector has grown since. Business counts include branches of chains, so they measure local competition rather than independent owners. Distances are straight-line between area centres.
